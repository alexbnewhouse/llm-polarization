# Compute budget: dialogues x turns x tok/s = wall-clock days

Written 2026-08-25 from measurements on the box, not spec sheets. Updated
2026-09-08 with the Olmo-3-7B re-measurement. Source: Notion task "Write the
compute budget". The live numbers and the serving recipe are in
`models/RUN_APPROACH.md`; this file records the reasoning.

## The operating point

llama.cpp Vulkan, `-np 8`, q8_0 KV cache, `--cache-reuse 256`,
`-c = (depth + 1024) * slots`.

Measured on qwen3.6:35b-a3b (`models/parallel_scaling.csv`): **116.4 tok/s
aggregate at 32k depth**, which is 2.29x the naive single-stream default
(50.9 tok/s at np=1, f16 KV). np=16 is slower than np=8 (94.0 tok/s), so do
not over-subscribe.

## Cost per arm

Assumptions: 2,700 dialogues per arm, 20 turns, 200 tokens per turn, both dyad
roles generated by the arm's model. That is 21.6 M generated tokens per arm,
so days = 21.6e6 / (aggregate tok/s) / 86,400 = 250 / tok/s.

| Arm | tok/s at 32k, np=8, q8_0 | Days per arm | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| qwen3.6:35b-a3b | 116.4 | 2.15 | measured 2026-08-25 |
| gpt-oss:20b | 83.4 | 3.0 | **measured 2026-09-08** on the HIP build (no Vulkan path for MXFP4); was ~98 / ~2.6 extrapolated |
| glm-4.7-flash | 29.1 | 8.6 | **measured 2026-09-08** on the HIP build (Vulkan 25.9); was ~60 / ~4.5 extrapolated. Weakest arm; prefill ~250 tok/s |
| Olmo-3-7B-Instruct | 41.5 | 6.0 | **measured 2026-09-08** (was ~41 / ~6.3 days extrapolated from 18.1 x 2.29; single-stream is faster than assumed, parallel factor only 1.47x, the two cancel) |

The 2026-08-25 four-arm total was about 15.6 days against 21 allocated to the
waves (W7 to W9). At the recommended 40-turn dialogues it was about 26 days,
still inside the window once the pilot and baseline are excluded.

## Sensitivity

| Turns per dialogue | Days per arm (qwen3.6) | Four-arm total (2026-08-25 basis) |
|---|---|---|
| 20 (original plan) | 2.15 | ~15.6 |
| 40 (**chosen 2026-09-10**) | 4.3 | ~26 |
| 60 | 6.4 | ~36 |

**40 turns is decided** (`docs/decisions/persona-stability.md`), and on the
measured 2026-09-08 basis it does not fit with four arms: ~39.5 days against
the 21 allocated to W7-W9. Dropping glm-4.7-flash brings it to ~22.3. The turn
count and the arm count are therefore one decision, not two.

## Caveats

- All four arms are measured at the operating point as of 2026-09-08. The
  four-arm total is about 19.8 days at 20 turns (fits the 21-day wave window)
  and about 40 at 40 turns (does not; dropping glm-4.7-flash gives about 22).
  Parallel factors at np=8: qwen3.6 2.29x, gpt-oss 1.6x, Olmo 1.47x, glm
  1.29x. The ollama blobs for gpt-oss and glm do not load in upstream
  llama.cpp; the ggml-org GGUFs do. gpt-oss needs the HIP build.
- The single largest lever is **KV cache reuse**: a turn at 32k prefills a
  handful of tokens, not 32,768. If the harness loses it, every number here
  is wrong by about three orders of magnitude. Log `prompt_n` on every call.
- Olmo-3 uses sliding-window attention; llama.cpp disables `--cache-reuse`
  (chunk-level reuse) for it but exact-prefix caching still works. Verify in
  the pilot that the reinforced-persona reminder (appended after the history)
  does not force a full re-prefill on the seeker side.
