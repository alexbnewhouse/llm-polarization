## The grid

Every mentor arm runs the same grid. Model is a run-level factor (one
`llama-server` per arm, recorded in `manifest.json`), not a per-dyad key.

| Factor | Levels | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| `topic` | `immigration_enforcement`, `decarbonization` | fixed, crossed |
| `ideology` | `strong_left`, `lean_left`, `moderate`, `lean_right`, `strong_right` | fixed, crossed |
| `openness` | `open`, `closed` | fixed, crossed |
| `role` | three congruent persona variants per ideology level | nested replicate, random effect |
| control | `ideology: none`, `openness: null`, `role: null` | two cells, one per topic |

| Per mentor arm | Cells | N per cell | Dialogues |
|---|---|---|---|
| Treated: 2 topics x 5 ideology x 2 openness | 20 | 135 (45 per variant) | 2,700 |
| Control: 2 topics, no persona | 2 | 135 | 270 |
| **Total** | **22** | | **2,970** |

Three arms: **8,910 wave dialogues**. Days at the measured operating point
(`models/RUN_APPROACH.md`, 40 turns, scaled by 2,970/2,700): qwen3.6 4.7,
gpt-oss 6.6, Olmo 13.2, **about 24.5 days** against 21 allocated to W7-W9.
The 3.5-day overrun is absorbed by W6 (machine-only) and the W14 buffer; it
was accepted knowingly rather than paid for by cutting the control.

**The days figure carries a stale assumption.** Every days-per-arm number in
`compute-budget.md`, `model-arm.md` and `RUN_APPROACH.md` assumes "both dyad
roles generated by the arm's model". Since 2026-09-08 the seeker is one fixed
model from a different family, so in every arm the mentor's half of the tokens
runs on the arm's model and the seeker's half runs on the seeker's, on the same
GPU. Per arm that is roughly (mentor half / arm tok/s) + (seeker half / seeker
tok/s): the three mentor halves alone are about 12.3 days, and the seeker
halves add 3 x (2,970 x 40 x 200 / seeker tok/s / 86,400) days. A seeker at
~100 tok/s makes the total about 20.5 days; one at ~30 tok/s makes it about
40. The grid does not depend on this, but the seeker choice does: its
throughput is a compute criterion, not only the tie-break that
`persona-stability.md` Q2 names. Recompute the budget when the pilot has
measured the chosen seeker, and treat 24.5 as the like-for-like figure until
then.

## Why these levels

**Five ideology levels, not two.** Every note since 2026-09-02 says "the two
ideology arms", but the compute budget was always priced on the five-level
grid: 2 x 5 x 3 x 3 = 90 cells x 30 is where 2,700 dialogues per arm comes
from. Five levels turn the headline test from a difference into a
dose-response curve, and they make tornberg2026audits' accommodation account
a *shape* prediction rather than a rival explanation (below). Stance anchors
are aligned with ideology: a `strong_right` persona is pro-militarization and
decarbonization-skeptical. Crossing identity against stance was considered
and rejected as a doubling of cells for a secondary question.

**Openness at two levels.** The persona template drafts exactly two
`openness_text` variants and a distinct middle text is hard to write. The Oct
19 cut list's "collapse openness to two" is therefore pre-spent; the list is
re-sequenced below.

**Role as nested variants, not a crossed factor.** Three congruent roles per
ideology level, so the ideology effect is not confounded with one persona's
idiosyncrasies (stimulus sampling; analyse `role` as a random effect within
`ideology`). Crossing ideologically coded roles against stated ideology would
give a direct cue-versus-stance test, but it would mean 60 cells, 30 personas
many of them incongruent and hard to hold for 40 turns, and it is not needed
for the paper's claim. Recorded as future work. **The role slugs are not
frozen here**: the grid fixes the count (three per level); the slugs are
registered when the personas are written (Notion task "(2/2) Draft the
modular persona prompt template") and the randomizer reads them from the
persona catalogue.

**The control is bare.** No name, no role, no openness: the seeker's system
prompt carries only the advisor-framing opening request for the topic. It runs
in reinforced mode with a framing-only reminder, so the mechanics (a trailing
system message on every seeker turn, the same KV-cache pattern) are identical
to the treated cells. Adherence scoring does not apply to it. It is sized at
one treated cell per topic. "Persona minus stance" (keep role and openness,
strip the anchors) was rejected: the roles still carry an inferred cue, so it
does not isolate what the control is for.

## Three consequences of five levels

1. **`moderate` is not the control.** A moderate persona still has a role (an
   inferred cue) and an explicit centrist statement (a stated cue). The bare
   control is the zero point; `moderate` is a treated level.
2. **The mentor `alignment` metric needs a scoreable moderate.** "How far does
   the line agree with the persona's position" has no answer for "I see both
   sides." Moderate anchors are therefore written as *specific* centrist
   positions (for example: enforcement at the border, no troops in cities),
   not as ambivalence. The persona template task owns the wording; the
   confounder-balance check covers word count and anchor count across all five
   levels (nudo2025exaggeration).
3. **Pre-registered shape test against accommodation.** tornberg2026audits:
   models accommodate the interlocutor they infer, with an ~8x asymmetry
   (conservative-coded askers move responses far more than progressive-coded
   ones). Accommodation therefore predicts mentor movement that is large for
   the two right levels, small for the two left, and near zero in the bare
   control. Movement that is roughly symmetric in ideology and monotone in
   intensity supports genuine dialogue-driven drift. Either result is a
   finding.

## Persona inventory the grid implies

15 backstories (5 levels x 3 variants), 10 anchor sets (5 levels x 2 topics),
30 persona texts, 60 seeker system prompts once the openness line is swapped
in, plus 2 control prompts. This, not compute, is the bottleneck before the
Sep 18 pilot.

## The pilot under this grid

20 cells x 5 dyads x 2 delivery modes = 200, plus 2 control cells x 5 = 10,
per candidate seeker model. The 200 matches the Dialogues property on the
pilot task, which was set before the freeze.

## The Oct 19 cut list, re-sequenced

The first item ("drop the third model arm") was spent on 2026-09-10. The
second ("collapse openness to two") is pre-spent here. The list is now:

1. **N per cell 135 -> 90** (30 per variant). Loses power, keeps every factor.
2. **Collapse ideology to three levels**: `strong_left`, `moderate`,
   `strong_right`. Removes 40% of treated cells and keeps the shape test.
3. **Drop one topic.** Keep whichever produced cleaner pilot transcripts.

Never cut Olmo; never cut the control.

## The `condition` schema

```json
{"topic": "immigration_enforcement", "ideology": "lean_right", "openness": "open", "role": "rural_rancher"}
{"topic": "immigration_enforcement", "ideology": "none", "openness": null, "role": null}
```

Four keys, always present. `ideology` is new as of this freeze. `role` is the
persona variant slug. `test_grid.py` checks `harness/dyads.example.jsonl`
against `prompts/grid.json`; the randomizer must produce rows that pass the
same check.
